# Cedar dispatch rollout retrospective

**The retained records establish one rollback handoff gap before the checklist revision. They cannot establish whether the gap recurred less often afterward.** This retrospective covers the closed rollout from November 10 at 08:00 UTC to November 13 at 08:00 UTC. Its completeness remains unverified because the collector data, operational traces, and independent review are unavailable.

## What happened

On November 10 at 20:58 UTC, the backup operator reported that their approval packet lacked the rollback command. At 21:10 UTC, the nightly review recorded a `FAIL` on that omission. The reviewer asked the operator for the command, and publication was held until the operator supplied it. These records describe one episode. The review records that the command was supplied, while the backup operator’s eventual receipt and the final publication outcome remain unverified. The duration of the hold is unknown. [`console.txt`, line 14 and `review-n10.txt`, November 10 21:10 UTC](TASK.md)

The immediate failure was a missing command in the packet used for the backup handoff. The backup operator detected the gap, and the nightly review enforced the publication hold. The older checklist survives only as a diff, and the task and approval traces are unavailable. The evidence cannot establish how the omission entered the packet, which earlier check applied, or why it did not catch the omission. No systemic cause can be assigned. [`archive-index.txt`, `checklist.diff`, `console.txt`, line 14 and `review-n10.txt`](TASK.md)

On November 11 at 11:00 UTC, the approved checklist revision required the rollback command to be given to the backup operator before publication. On November 12 at 13:20 UTC, a prepare hook recorded `PREVIEW_COMPLETE` on the dry-run queue. At 13:21 UTC, the task owner reported that the checklist edit was in the shared repository and that the runner printout was missing from the export. The approval verifies the wording change. The records leave open when the revised checklist governed a real publication, whether anyone checked the handoff, and whether the backup operator received the command. [`checklist.diff` and `console.txt`, lines 32–33](TASK.md)
