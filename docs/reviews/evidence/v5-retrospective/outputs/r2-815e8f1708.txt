## What happened

On November 10 at 20:58 UTC, the backup operator reported that the approval packet lacked the rollback command. At 21:10, the nightly review recorded a `FAIL` on the same handoff. The reviewer requested the command and held publication until the operator supplied it. These records show one gap and one failed review, with two accounts of the same omission. The timestamps are 12 minutes apart. The hold duration, the backup operator’s eventual receipt of the command, and the final publication outcome are unknown. [`console.txt`, line 14; `review-n10.txt`, November 10 entry]

The missing command prompted the reviewer’s intervention. The records do not establish who prepared the packet, why it omitted the command, which control was in force, or whether an earlier check ran. The review caught the omission before publication. The missing packet and earlier checklist snapshot prevent a deeper causal finding.

On November 11 at 11:00 UTC, the revision was approved. It requires giving the rollback command to the backup operator before publication. On November 12 at 13:20 UTC, the prepare hook reported `PREVIEW_COMPLETE` with `queue=dry-run`. One minute later, the task owner reported that the edit was in the shared repo and the runner printout was missing from the export. Approval establishes the wording change. Live adoption and effectiveness remain unverified. [`checklist.diff`, approved revision; `console.txt`, lines 32–33]

The review addressed the immediate publication hold by obtaining the command from the operator. It leaves the actual handoff unresolved in the record. The rollout’s delivered result is unverified. The development process has one confirmed publication hold caused by an incomplete handoff packet. The checklist revision provides a preventive rule, with no verified evidence that it took effect in the live publication path.

## Problem and remediation

| ID | Confirmed occurrence | Status and confidence |
|---|---|---|
| P1 | The November 10 approval packet omitted the rollback command, requiring reviewer intervention and a publication hold. | Moderate observed severity based on the hold. High confidence in the omission and intervention. Systemic cause, upstream checks, and delay are unknown. The operator supplied the command to the reviewer, but backup receipt is unverified. The preventive rule was approved. Live enforcement and effectiveness remain unverified. |

**R1 — Verify the handoff in the existing publication review.** The rollout maintainer should check the checklist version in use for the next comparable live publication and have the reviewer confirm that the backup operator received the applicable rollback command before authorizing publication. If the review already captures that confirmation, use the existing evidence. If it does not, add the confirmation to the existing review. This is a priority before the next comparable publication and has low implementation complexity.

1. **Verify the control and measure subsequent handoffs.** The Cedar dispatch maintainer owns this step, with the publication reviewer and backup operator providing the evidence. It has no prerequisite step. Confirm the active checklist and check that the publication review holds approval if the packet contains a command but backup receipt is missing. This uses the existing review path and excludes historical rework and new tooling. For subsequent comparable live publications, retain each eligible handoff’s result at the first review, including whether a gap required a hold and whether it was corrected before publication. Report missing records separately. Measure recurrence as gaps found at first review divided by eligible live handoffs with known results. Use equivalent evidence on both sides of a change before claiming a reduction. Future records can establish a prospective rate, while the historical comparison remains unavailable unless comparable records are recovered.
