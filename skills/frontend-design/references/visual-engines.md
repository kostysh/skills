# Visual Engines

Use this reference after choosing a surface mode. Pick one primary engine and optionally one secondary engine. Do not combine multiple engines with equal weight.

## `image-led`

Use when the page needs narrative realism, place, product desirability, editorial tone, or lifestyle atmosphere.

Best for:

- brands
- venues
- editorial storytelling
- hospitality
- fashion
- product launches with strong photography

Guidance:

- lead with one dominant image
- reserve calm tonal space for text
- crop decisively
- use texture and overlays only to support the image

## `type-led`

Use when the brand, message, or voice should carry the page more than imagery.

Best for:

- strong brand systems
- editorial brands
- manifesto-style pages
- posters
- minimalist launches

Guidance:

- make scale and spacing do the heavy lifting
- pick typography with character
- keep color disciplined
- avoid decorative clutter that competes with the text system

## `grid-led`

Use when the layout itself should communicate discipline, rhythm, or structure.

Best for:

- portfolios
- magazines
- editorial systems
- exhibition pages
- more conceptual brand work

Guidance:

- establish a strong alignment logic
- use asymmetry deliberately, not randomly
- let spacing and proportion create interest
- keep modules few and distinct

## `product-led`

Use when product screenshots, workflows, interactions, or interface states are the narrative anchor.

Best for:

- SaaS pages
- app launches
- feature storytelling
- redesign showcases

Guidance:

- make the product the proof
- avoid turning screenshots into floating dashboard clutter
- show one decisive workflow or state before showing variety
- keep supporting copy short and concrete

## `data-led`

Use when the work is primarily about metrics, scanning, comparison, operations, or monitoring.

Best for:

- dashboards
- admin tools
- analytics surfaces
- operator consoles

Guidance:

- emphasize hierarchy, labels, and grouping
- keep accents scarce and meaningful
- remove decoration that slows scanning
- prefer layout over wrapper cards

## Selection Rules

- If imagery is essential to meaning, choose `image-led`.
- If wording and brand voice must dominate, choose `type-led`.
- If structure itself should create the visual identity, choose `grid-led`.
- If screenshots or workflows prove the value, choose `product-led`.
- If operators need to scan, compare, and act, choose `data-led`.

If two engines seem equally important, choose the one that should dominate the first screen and use the other only as support.
